## 0. Elevator pitch

A sailor shipwrecked on a beautiful, wrong island searches for **The Forge** — the
legendary place where broken things are made whole — and slowly uncovers why the
island of *Fruits* is really a scar: the healed-over wound of a catastrophe nobody
is allowed to remember. **The Great Deletion** erased people, places and memories,
and the island *regrew wrong*: biomes from different eras smashed together like
pieces of different puzzles forced into one box.

Tone: **serene surface, rot underneath**. Dry humor is allowed and encouraged —
the horror lands *because* everyone is so pleasant about it. Think of a warm
fireplace in a house where the stairs lead nowhere.

Core themes (mandated):
1. **Mystery & Memory** — what was deleted, who remembers, and how memory can be a place.
2. **Lost Connections & Betrayal** — the Guild of Fruits broke; friends deleted each other.
3. **Discovery & Choice** — the player eventually chooses what the island becomes.

## 1. The world

### 1.1 Fruits Island (surface name) / Fructasia (old, true name)
- The island **once belonged to a guild called "the Fruits"**: seven custodians,
  each sworn to one of seven *Fruits*. A Fruit is **not a fruit** — it is a living
  memory-vessel, a fruit-shaped reliquary holding one of the island's seven
  foundational memories (the Sea, the Harvest, the Fire, the Song, the Snow, the
  Labyrinth, and the Deletion itself).
- Custodians carried fruit motifs; the common folk painted fruit on everything.
  That is why the island is *drowning* in fruit imagery. The fruit motif is a
  fossil of faith.
- **The Great Deletion** (≈ 40 years before the game): an event no one can
  describe because description requires memory. Whole districts, whole families,
  and one of the seven Fruits were *removed*. The island's mind healed over the
  gap the way skin heals over a splinter — and the splinter is still in there.
  The smashed-together biomes (volcano beside snow beside neon city) are scar
  tissue: eras and places that no longer agree on *when* they are.
- The Guild is gone. Survivors' descendants live in **Bouga Bouga Village** and
  pretend the past is a rumor. The island hums, quietly, all the time. Most
  people have learned not to hear it.
- **The Forge**: a legend sailors chase — the only place said to be able to
  *rebuild what was deleted*. Nobody has ever returned from finding it. The
  storm that wrecked the Sailor was not weather: **the island pulls seekers of
  the Forge toward itself**, like a lamp pulling moths. Whether that is mercy or
  hunger is the question of the whole game.

### 1.2 The seven Fruits and their keepers (deep lore, spoil in late game)
| Fruit | Memory | Keeper (roadmap boss) | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Firstfruit (Sea) | the water that agreed to hold the island | lost with the Deletion | The Thread (LAB) |
| The Harvestfruit | growth without limit | **Tomato** (overgrown ritual fruit) | Sacrification Volcano |

### 1.3 Key characters (locked designs & arcs)
- **The Sailor (player)** — quiet, dry-humored, shipwrecked. Chasing The Forge for
  a reason they do not discuss in Chapter One (a person they lost; revealed late:
  the Sailor's own memory has a deleted piece — the island pulled them because
  *they are part of what was taken*). Blue peacoat, red neckerchief, white cap.
- **Vax** — young man, self-appointed "leader" of Bouga Bouga Village. Messy dark
  brown hair, pale complexion, **one bright red eye (right) and one grey eye** —
  the red eye never blinks. Thick red scarf with one long end hanging over his
  chest. Black long-sleeved jacket over a dark charcoal shirt, baggy light grey
  trousers, red-and-white sneakers (red dominant). Loud confidence hiding deep
  uncertainty. Truth (late game): Vax is **Goussi's younger brother-figure and
  the only person who watched the Deletion begin and kept his memories** — the
  red eye is a "Deletion burn": he looked directly at the erasure and it left a
  mark. He has spent his life pretending to be a harmless village leader so the
  LAB never comes for him. In Chapter 7 he is forced to choose between the
  Sailor and Goussi. In Chapter 1 he only *warns*: "storms don't usually steer."
- **Ali Sama** — tavern keeper of Bouga Bouga, warm, generous, deliberately
  forgets anything important ("Old houses talk. This one hums."). Knows more
  about the shrine than she admits. Gives the Sailor Torchlight Tea.
- **Majhool ("the Unknown")** — ancient, half-deaf archivist who sits at the Old
  Fruit Shrine. Speaks in quotes from books that no longer exist. Keeper of the
  last honest account of the Guild. Gives the **Shrine Token**. His name
  literally means "unknown" — he named himself that after the Deletion took his.
- **Levi** — a child who "remembers" things that never happened, including the
  Sailor. Sweet and quietly terrifying. ("You drowned. But not here. Not yet.")
  Late-game: Levi is a *memory bud* of the Labyrinthfruit — the island growing a
  new keeper.
- **Goussi** (Chapter 7 antagonist) — the LAB's keeper; engineered the "stitching"
  that keeps the smashed eras from tearing apart. Believes the Deletion can be
  *finished properly* — a clean, painless island. Vax's oldest friend.
- **Ahmed AD** (Chapter 5) — a robotic archivist built *before* the Deletion,
  still recording. Patient, deadpan, kind. Assists in Chapter 7.
- **Hadi & Khaled** (Chapter 4) — neon city twins: one is the singer, one is the
  song. Boss battle is a rhythm duel.
- **Ishak** (Chapter 6) — keeper of Monimonia, the purple memory-labyrinth.
  Tests whether the Sailor can hold onto themselves.
- **Murad** (Chapter 8) — the last custodian, living in Truthobia, the place
  where the Deletion is *still happening*. Offers the final choice.

### 1.4 Geography (locked, in visit order)
1. **The Beach** — serene, eerie. Wreckage, gulls that stop singing, circles
   pressed in the sand that the tide refuses to wash away, an omen of pale eyes
   in the treeline. The "collecting shipwrecks again" line lives here.
2. **The Jungle Trail** — the only safe-ish path east; carved faces in the fruit,
   a milestone with half its inscription chiseled away. Encounters begin here.
3. **Bouga Bouga Village** — rustic, decaying hub. Tavern, well, notice board,
   the Old Fruit Shrine (seven niches, six empty, one holding a dull token),
   a welded-shut hut, the north gate to the volcano (closed "because the
   mountain is indisposed").
4. **Sacrification Volcano** — ritual grounds; the Tomato boss; the Harvestfruit.
5. **Epilepsy City** — neon-corrupted streets that strobe with someone else's
   memories; Hadi & Khaled.
6. **Deserted Snows** — frozen laboratories; Ahmed AD; things preserved mid-step.
7. **Monimonia** — purple surreal labyrinth of memory-distortion; Ishak.
8. **The Thread (LAB)** — high-tech underground; Goussi; the confrontation.
9. **Truthobia** — final area; Murad; the player's choice about the island's fate.

## 2. Chapter One — "The First Slice" (this build)

Beat sheet (locked):
1. **Intro panels** (9 panels): The Forge legend → the Sailor's long search → the
   storm that behaved like a hand → the ship destroyed → cold, sand, gulls → an
   island not on any chart, fruit-heavy, bright-eyed, *wrong*.
2. **The Beach**: wake among the wreckage. Explore; find the Memory Lantern
   (rest/save); read the sign ("PLEASE DO NOT FEED THE ISLAND. This is not a
   joke."); find the sigil and the three-toed prints; the rusted Ship's Bell in
   the sand (key item, optional); the omen eyes in the treeline.
3. **Vax encounter**: he knows the Sailor was seeking The Forge ("Sailors with
   that particular face are always looking for The Forge"). He warns: "Storms
   don't usually steer." He is the leader nobody listens to. He points east:
   follow the trail to Bouga Bouga Village, ask for Ali Sama. He gives a
   **Map Shard** (key item). He forbids the north path after dark (the cave
   mouth stays sealed — Chapter 2+).
4. **Jungle Trail**: first real encounters (Crab Apple, Bramblett; Gullgazer
   rare). Feedback on entering: the birds stop singing. Scripted first encounter
   mid-trail. Milestone with chiseled-away inscription.
5. **Bouga Bouga Village**: Ali Sama (tavern) gives Torchlight Tea; Levi by the
   well remembers the Sailor from dreams; Majhool at the shrine explains the
   seven niches and the Deletion ("that's not its name. Names are for things
   that end.") and gives the **Shrine Token**.
6. **Chapter end**: with Token in hand, the north gate hums faintly; chapter
   card: *END OF CHAPTER ONE — the mountain is indisposed. It will not stay
   that way.* Save prompt at the village Memory Lantern.

Optional chapter-1 flavor beats (all locked into the build):

- Nadia the villager: "Lovely harvest. Same as last year. And before that."
- The welded hut: welded *from the inside*.

## 3. Rules the writing must obey (tone lock)

1. The island is beautiful first, wrong second. Never lead with the horror.
2. Nobody explains the Deletion in Chapter One. They deflect, warmly.
3. Vax is comic in delivery, tragic in content. Never let him monolog.
4. The Sailor speaks in short, dry, understated lines. Max ~12 words usually.
5. Humor = understatement + specificity ("three coconuts and a wooden spoon").
6. The word "Fructasia" appears exactly twice in Chapter One (shrine, Majhool).
7. Violence is rare and short; dread is constant and quiet.
8. Every item description must contain one concrete sensory detail + one
   "wrongness" (subtle). No generic fantasy flavor text.
9. Sparing enemies vs. hurting them is tracked (`flags.spared`, `flags.hurt`) —
   it will matter in later chapters (Discovery & Choice). Never punish yet.
10. Never copy Undertale's assets, scenes, or specific lines. Inspired in
    *structure* (bullet-box mercy system, typewriter, quiet dread), original in
    every particular. No meta fourth-wall breaking in Chapter One.

## 4. Systems canon (how mechanics map to lore)

- **HP** = grip on yourself. The island dulls people; damage is *forgetting yourself*.
- **Memory Lanterns** = rest points. They are old Guild lamps that remember
  travelers; saving = letting the lamp remember you (so defeat = the lamp
  re-kindles you at "the last moment you were entirely yourself").
- **ACT / MERCY** = talking an island-thing down. Most monsters are scar tissue:
  fragments of deleted memories wearing a shape. Sparing = letting them settle.
- **The battle "soul"** = the Sailor's lantern-light (a small flame, not a heart).
- **Items**: island fruit heals but tastes "like a memory of sweetness" (hook for
  Chapter 6). Torchlight Tea clears the head (full heal early game).
- **Equipment**: the Ship's Bell (accessory, DEF+1 — "it still points home"),
  Worn Gloves (ATK+1, found on the trail), Sailor's Cap (starter, DEF+0? no —
  DEF is 1 base; cap is cosmetic-key item, see items doc in TECH_NOTES).
